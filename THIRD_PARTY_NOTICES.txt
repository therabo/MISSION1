NASA model and imagery

- ISS 3D model: NASA Visualization Technology Applications and Development (VTAD),
  International Space Station 3D Model: https://science.nasa.gov/resource/international-space-station-3d-model/.
  The shipped GLB preserves the source geometry and lossless texture content, with
  deduplication, semantic feature metadata and sampler settings. Source/runtime hashes
  and the historical configuration caveats remain in the
  model manifest: frontend/src/cesium/issModelManifest.json. Pirs is historical model
  geometry, not a claim that the current station still has that module.
- Star map: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio,
  Tycho Catalog Sky Map: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/3572/. The local six-face skybox is
  a projected and tonally processed derivative. The exact source, processing, credit,
  per-face hashes and reuse reference remain in the
  skybox manifest: frontend/src/cesium/skyboxManifest.json.
- Earth imagery: NASA Blue Marble: Next Generation and NASA Black Marble 2016
  historical composite, supplied through NASA GIBS: https://nasa-gibs.github.io/gibs-api-docs/,
  part of NASA ESDIS. The bundled night fallback is NASA's
  2016 Earth-at-Night JPEG: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/imagerecords/144000/144898/BlackMarble_2016_01deg.jpg.
  These are historical visualization assets, not live lights or weather.

NASA is acknowledged as the source under its
Images and Media Usage Guidelines: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/.
NASA marks contained in source geometry are not MISSION1 branding. Third-party
content, agency marks and identifiable people may have separate usage restrictions;
the source guidelines continue to apply.
