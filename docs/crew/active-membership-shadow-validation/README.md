## Identity, membership and temporal policy

A shadow human is an existing typed LL2 provider anchor with its original MISSION1 UUID and profile snapshot. An LL2 assignment needs no cross-source match. NASA slots require AUTO_MATCH in the pinned V9 graph, the exact source table/reference/hash, compatible source head and the corresponding LL2 component. DEFER, SEPARATE, missing/changed/revoked bindings remain explicit unresolved source slots. No frontend name, profile-existence test, fuzzy fallback or V7 reviewed decision enters the automatic policy. V7 is consulted only by a separate diagnostic comparison after materialization.

At evaluation time T, **ACTIVE = at least one eligible fresh positive support, unless a completed named departure closes the operational episode without a strictly later qualified operational positive**. Supports remain separate records: LL2_ASSIGNMENT, NASA_EDITORIAL_ROSTER, NASA_OPERATIONAL_POSITIVE and QUALIFIED_REENTRY. Assignment and editorial membership never imply physical presence. A qualified operational-only visitor may be active with no Expedition assignment. Known humans without eligible support are RETAINED; their identity, metadata, career, biography, media, aliases and history remain in the original source/profile tables.

A successfully selected complete omission retracts only that source's support. It never creates a departure. A failed acquisition preserves last-good context and independent health, rather than synthesizing an empty successful roster. Stale supports remain diagnostic but cannot alone sustain ACTIVE. Completed departures use only already classified retained evidence; their episode-closing effect survives expiry of fresh presence confirmation. Page/assignment refresh cannot reopen that episode. Later qualified operational reentry/current-positive evidence may reopen it; equal-time positive/departure contradiction is ambiguous and fails closed. Event precision remains unspecified when the source supplies no precision; publication fallback is explicitly PUBLICATION_TIME_PROXY. The operational RSS parser is unchanged.

Freshness reuses `crew-freshness-v1`: LL2 6h; successful operational verification 6h; current operational assertions 72h; entry/departure presence implications 14d. The explicit configurable shadow policy adds editorial roster 6h, editorial topic 6h and personal-resource health 7d. Personal resources never supply membership support. Capture must precede T; CMS modified, page publication and Expedition start never backdate roster eligibility or nullable personal assignment validity. V9 identity freshness means pinned revision/source compatibility plus independent pending/failed-job health, rather than an arbitrary expiring identity TTL.

## Persistence and execution

V10 adds eight tables: `crew_active_projection_revision`, `_member`, `_support`, `_slot`, `_finding`, `_evidence`, `_input_capture` and `_work`. Seven history tables reject UPDATE/DELETE; children can only be inserted in the parent revision's creation transaction, sealing history after commit. `_work` is the sole mutable singleton. There are restrictive source/profile/assignment/roster/subject/decision/observation/document FKs, source ownership/time checks, exact serialized-versus-relational input pins, SHA-256 checks, a unique root/one-successor predecessor chain, state indexes and a deferred complete-projection check. ACTIVE requires an eligible support; member vetoes and operational supports must belong to the pinned evidence cut. No cascading destruction is configured.

One advisory transaction lock precedes the loader's separate read-only repeatable-read transaction. It obtains a coherent source/graph/health cut through batched SELECTs, without per-member queries. Atomic inserts preserve predecessor lineage and A→B→A acquisitions. Same cut, policy and eligibility return the existing revision without duplicate rows. Source/graph triggers transactionally increment durable work generation; completion acknowledges only the observed generation. A bounded scheduled worker (15m initial delay, 1m fixed delay, configurable minimum 1m) also evaluates expiry when no source changes occur. Failed transactions roll back completely and increment an independent failure counter. Restart retains pending work.

`ActiveMembershipStore.latest()` and `health()` are internal SELECT-only queries. `LegacyMembershipComparison.read()` obtains the shadow result first, then maps original LL2 anchors through V7 for comparison. Neither feeds the other. Public GET has no lifecycle dependency and performs no materialization, scoring, refresh or provider acquisition.

## Counts, findings and health

Resolved active humans, retained humans, unresolved active/retained slots, LL2 assignments, editorial slots, V9-resolved editorial memberships, website editorial count, operational official current count and last-known operational count are distinct dimensions. Unresolved slots never acquire UUIDs or increase human count. Null operational count stays unavailable; website count never replaces it. Count coherence does not establish agreement between human sets. Partial NASA identity coverage is reported as membership unverified rather than falsely claiming omission.

Findings use separate MEMBERSHIP, COUNT, SOURCE_FIELD, SOURCE_HEALTH and IDENTITY namespaces. Source-field role/agency disagreements are preserved independently. HEALTHY/DEGRADED/UNAVAILABLE projection health exposes the underlying stream outcomes/freshness, coverage, pending jobs and disagreements. A healthy editorial roster never heals malformed operational evidence. With no eligible source state, a zero supported-human result is UNAVAILABLE, not a claim of an empty physical station.
