# ISS Crew event-aware refresh

Design recorded before implementation, 2026-10-03. Baseline: `4c7959edaa37726cbdb2c9e44c4d0c585bdbabfc`. Scope is Crew scheduling, observation history and operational audit. Orbit runtime, Crew REST JSON, NASA parser/reconciler and frontend runtime remain frozen. ADR-0010 records the significant scheduling decision.

## Verified source contract

LL2 2.3.0 `/events/` is the bounded planning source: `spacestation__ids=4&type__ids=2,8&date__gte=<UTC now-2d>&date__lte=<UTC now+30d>&mode=detailed&ordering=date&limit=20`. Station/type filtering, these exact IDs and time filters were verified from the provider's browsable API and a real 200 JSON response on 2026-10-03. One page, no pagination or per-event/launch/person enrichment. A non-null `next`, truncated count, duplicate IDs, wrong station/type/mode, malformed timestamps or invalid relations fails closed; it cannot establish a complete calendar.

The real response contained three events, including future undocking and docking. `date` is a planning date; `last_updated` is provider metadata. `date_precision` specifies schedule precision. There is no event-level status or crew/cargo boolean in this representation. Astronaut relations were empty for all three observations. These real records are UNKNOWN, never inferred from names, mission descriptions, launch success, agency or vehicle family. A nonempty validated event `astronauts` relation supports CREWED scheduling relevance only; it establishes neither station presence nor a completed transfer. The adapter currently cannot establish CARGO or CANCELLED from this contract. These domain states permit explicit future verified inputs and test policy behavior; production does not fabricate them. Seconds/minutes precision permits a window; null/day/month/year precision does not.

`/docking_events/` describes actual past docking occurrences, not a reliable upcoming calendar. Its verified station filter is `space_station_target__id`, whereas `space_station` was silently ignored in a research probe. It is not the production planning source. No launch endpoint is needed. Published LL2 unauthenticated quota is 15 requests/hour ([provider](https://thespacedevs.com/llapi)). Exact captures and metadata are in the validation dossier.

NASA continues using the official Space Station RSS feed, existing parser v1.0.0, conditional ETag/Last-Modified and 304 reuse. Publication, explicit event time and retrieval time remain separate. Planned articles and counts do not cause roster acceleration. No browser scrape, per-article enrichment or new parser grammar is introduced.

## Policy and budget

An injected Clock drives a pure policy. Baseline LL2 roster and NASA verification remain 2h. Calendar discovery is 6h; its initial delay is 15m. A fresh complete catalog (maximum age 12h) allows anticipation in `[scheduled-6h, scheduled+6h]`, inclusive. CREWED shortens NASA to 30m; UNKNOWN to 1h; CARGO keeps 2h. Calendar failure disables planning acceleration; last successful observations remain history. A reschedule uses the latest successful catalog association; disappearance stops planning without inventing cancellation.

Completed named NASA PERSON_ENTERED_STATION or PERSON_DEPARTED_STATION evidence opens one durable follow-up per semantic evidence ID. Context is explicit event time, otherwise publication time; retrieval never supplies event time. Future or more than 24h old contexts are ineligible. Deadline is context+24h, with at most six LL2 actual attempts and a 1h minimum interval. Failed and interrupted IN_PROGRESS attempts consume this budget; skipped attempts do not. Duplicate articles, 304s and process restart cannot reopen a terminal follow-up. Counts, docking-only reports and planned assertions are inert. LL2-to-NASA reciprocal triggers are deliberately deferred: calendar anticipation is sufficient and one-way completed-evidence follow-up prevents ping-pong.

Event settlement requires every named subject to have a safe identity resolution, the corresponding reconciled person state to cite this evidence and agree with arrival/departure, and the LL2 assignment to agree (arrival assigned/departure absent), with a successful primary fetch after context. Unresolved identities and stale/ambiguous presence cannot settle. Equal counts and blanket MATCH/PARTIAL_MATCH are not settlement conditions. A later confirmed opposite assertion for every safely resolved subject closes the older transfer as SUPERSEDED. Horizon or attempt exhaustion returns to normal even while PARTIAL_MATCH persists.

Maximum defaults in 24h: baseline LL2 roster 12 + calendar 4 = 16; NASA 12. Continuous 12h CREWED window: NASA at most 31 (2h outside + 30m inside, conservative boundary allowance), UNKNOWN at most 24; one completed follow-up adds at most six LL2 attempts, so LL2 <=22. A deliberately conservative simulation also asserts hard bounds of LL2 <=28 and NASA <=48 across shifting/overlapping windows. The shared local LL2 ledger caps actual automated requests at 12 in any rolling hour, leaving three of the published 15 for other use; this cannot account for unrelated clients sharing the public quota. Provider Retry-After dominates every trigger and is shared across roster/catalog. Missing or invalid 429 Retry-After imposes the normal 2h cooldown.

## Execution, history and recovery

One coordinator ticks every minute and performs at most one due source per tick. Startup gates remain LL2 5m, NASA 10m, calendar 15m, including restarts. Persisted actual attempt times determine eligibility. Existing source services retain overlap guards, failure recording and transaction boundaries; they accept an explicit accelerated minimum interval only from the coordinator. Source-disabled settings suppress both baseline and accelerated calls. Disabling event-aware scheduling restores the accepted independent baseline schedulers.

V5 adds calendar runs, immutable event versions, run/version associations, bounded follow-ups and refresh triggers. Separate calendar runs avoid misrepresenting event discovery as successful roster ingestion. Calendar bodies/hashes/URI/API/adapter versions and receipt times are retained. Every due baseline or accelerated-intent decision records target, reason, event/evidence lineage, eligibility and the actual roster/NASA sync-run ID or calendar-run ID. The run and its trigger association commit before HTTP. Accelerated decisions are retained before eligibility, including during Retry-After. Eligibility in the audit is the creation-time decision; the policy and provider cooldown are reevaluated before execution. Decisions use a unique durable generation key; obsolete unclaimed decisions remain historical and are never blindly drained. Source calls and snapshots retain existing transactions. Event semantic A→B→A reuses immutable A while the new catalog run records its current selection.

A process guard prevents tick overlap; a PostgreSQL advisory lock prevents duplicate coordinator claims across callers. This is a single application, not a distributed scheduler. No startup reconciliation burst or automatic stale-run abandonment is introduced. An interrupted claimed IN_PROGRESS run stays inspectable, counts as an attempt and holds its durable cooldown. Its evidence follow-up remains bounded. Recovery never fabricates successful HTTP or settlement.

## Validation contract

Offline tests cover strict mapping, all event/time/classification branches, completed-evidence flow, event-specific settlement, rate limits, shared quota, startup and restart, deduplication, overlap, migration/rollback/history and fake-clock 24h HTTP request budgets. PostgreSQL 16.10 Testcontainers covers empty→V5 and V4→V5 with existing Crew/Orbit sentinels. Live checks are bounded and separated from default tests. Runtime validation covers PostgreSQL, packaged backend, all accepted Crew/Orbit routes and unchanged browser surfaces. Native touch remains unverified unless a genuine native-touch test is performed.
