# ADR-0015: Automatic Crew identity resolution, evidence graph, shadow decisions and self-correction

Status: Accepted for SHADOW only

Date: 2026-10-10

## Context

The automatic-identity and NASA Expedition investigations select a typed evidence graph with a frozen calibrated logistic model, personal corroboration, contradiction and ambiguity guards, and reversible automatic decisions. V8 already retains editorial resources, slots, captions and validated Biography relations. V7 remains the authoritative production identity projection. The research corpus does not certify the proposed deployment error budget.

## Decision

Keep one Java/JDBC/PostgreSQL Crew module. V9 adds an independent shadow overlay; V1–V8 migrations, reviewed decisions, UUID anchors, source histories and public Current/detail/Replay behavior remain authoritative and unchanged. An automatic decision uses actor MACHINE and method FROZEN_CALIBRATED_SHADOW. No promotion switch exists in this milestone.

Provider identity is a typed LL2 PERSON key with incarnation, or a NASA website resource, editorial slot or operational subject occurrence. Website IDs and slot locators are resource/mention identities, never universal personnel IDs. A source observation contains unfused, attributed facts and references to exact retained source versions/hashes/locators/capture times. Candidate pairs are NASA→LL2 retrieval hypotheses; only policy-qualified equality decisions contribute shadow graph edges. About-resource, name, roster, agency and mission relations remain evidence, never automatic union operations.

Use the exact `research-logistic-v1` thirteen coefficients and validation sigmoid from `metrics/model.json`, plus its frozen TF-IDF vocabulary/IDF. Track both artifacts unchanged, verify pinned SHA-256 before use, and serve natively in Java. Feature schema `research-13-v1-scoped-extractor-1` includes the research V2 alias/transliteration and class-of-year extraction, with explicit observation-kind qualification. Model and policy are immutable releases; every decision pins model/version/SHA, feature schema, policy, threshold/margin, exact vectors, raw/calibrated scores, source observation dependencies, runner-up, witnesses, negative checks, reasons and predecessor. No runtime Python, training, learned coefficient changes, LLM, embeddings or network calls are permitted in scoring.

BALANCED starts at probability ≥0.991, compatible name features, best ≥runner+0.20, qualified personal corroboration and no pair/component veto. Compare by addition to avoid a subtraction rounding artifact at the margin boundary. Precise DOB (qualified), selection in validated biography, shared person-scoped flight or lexical overlap >0.12 in a validated biography can corroborate. Generic Flight Engineer, Expedition number, same agency/nationality/roster/photo context cannot. Rejected or changed wrong-person targets never contribute positive resource evidence. A personal caption must explicitly name its own validated source slot; a shared-flight feature also needs independent LL2 person-scoped flight evidence. Missing detailed LL2 flight arrays are not invented from roster co-membership.

Hard contradictions remain explicit: authority-qualified precise DOB, proven different humans, incompatible provider/incarnation identities and whole-component conflicts veto matches. Secondary DOB disagreement is a scored conflict, not unconditional separation. AUTO_SEPARATE requires qualified different-human evidence; low probability alone defers. Strong LL2 catalogue keys are conservatively incompatible within an incarnation, while separate NASA CMS resources do not prove different humans. Component validation inspects the complete proposed union and active negative constraints before an edge is accepted.

AUTO_MATCH, AUTO_SEPARATE and AUTO_DEFER revisions are append-only, reversible and idempotent for the same pinned inputs/release. AUTO_DEFER requires no human: new relevant source evidence or an automatic retry re-evaluates it. Evidence changes can remove old corroboration, append a deferred/separate successor and rebuild the affected shadow component without deleting any raw observation or decision. Corrected negative evidence can likewise be superseded. Representatives are deterministic preferred LL2 debug keys, otherwise lexical resource keys; they are not public UUIDs. Graph revisions preserve component membership, representative, decision provenance and application lineage.

Transactional source INSERT notifications coalesce IMPORT work. A bounded set-query adapter selects retained successful captures (including A→B→A), then a source-fact index enqueues changed NASA identities, blocked counterparts, prior candidate dependencies and affected components. Candidate generation uses canonicalized exact names, native aliases/token blocks and person-scoped missions, capped at32; truncated blocks defer. The retained scope is bounded at10,000 identities and no external catalogue is fetched. A daily safety sweep catches missed notifications/release upgrades and week-old defers. It does not rescore an unchanged world catalogue each minute.

PostgreSQL jobs deduplicate work, claim with SKIP LOCKED and five-minute token leases, preserve generations arriving during a lease, and survive restart. Deferred retry is2h/6h/24h/7d; failed work backs off15m→2h and parks after8 failures until new evidence. Evaluation/head/revision writes serialize under a dedicated shadow advisory lock. No HTTP or enrichment occurs while holding that lock. Missing evidence is durable, automatically revisitable uncertainty; broader acquisition is a separate milestone.

Resolver health, model validity, queue age/backlog/failure, decision/revision/conflict counters and component/unbound gauges are independent of NASA/LL2 source health. Logs contain pair/release/probability/margin/witness/conflict/revision, never biographies. A separate read-only diagnostic service reads materialized shadow outputs before consulting V7; production knowledge is absent from candidate generation, extraction, scoring and corroboration. No fused canonical profile or reviewed manifest is an input. GET remains SELECT-only and network-free and never enqueues work.

## Alternatives and consequences

Name-only matching, hand-written weights, unguarded logistic scores, resource-ID transitivity and reuse of V7 human actor rows would either overstate identity evidence or create circular confirmation. A second service, broker, vector database, auxiliary crawler or frontend dedupe has no present need. Reusing retained source storage avoids parallel acquisition infrastructure; small shadow tables preserve production history and make downgrade a scheduler pause/application rollback without destructive migrations.

The current local source scope is sparse. Its LL2 observations have short biographies but no full flight arrays; the expected research five-slot match count is therefore not an acceptance constant. Some operational mentions remain deferred even when a separately retained biography matches. A typed subject→personal-resource verification adapter would be needed to qualify those mentions independently; V7 approvals cannot supply that proof. Unknown/colliding scripts without published aliases remain deferred. An indistinguishable synthetic homonym can still match identical observations; later contradiction can correct it, but unpublished distinctions cannot be inferred.

Production activation requires a separate accepted contract and rollout: independent blocked-candidate/person-disjoint cohorts and source/script coverage, false-merge confidence bounds, hard negatives, stable order/restart/concurrency, correction, Current lifecycle readiness, alias/Replay compatibility and rollback. Targets remain false human merges≤0.5%, persistent eligible splits≤10% after7days and eligible positive-link coverage≥90%. At least30days shadow with real turnover/failure/restarts is proposed, supplemented by sufficient independent accepted links (about598 zero-error links for a one-sided95%0.5% bound under independence). These gates are not met by this milestone.
