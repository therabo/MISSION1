NASA astronauts Jessica Watkins and Luke Delaney, CSA (Canadian Space Agency) astronaut Joshua Kutryk, and Roscosmos cosmonaut Sergey Teteryatnikov have entered the International Space Station following Dragon’s hatch opening at 9:19 p.m. EDT.

Crew-13 now joins Expedition 75 crew, which includes NASA astronauts Jessica Meir, Jack Hathaway, and Anil Menon, ESA (European Space Agency) astronaut Sophie Adenot, and Roscosmos cosmonauts Pyotr Dubrov, Anna Kikina, and Andrey Fedyaev already aboard the orbiting laboratory.
